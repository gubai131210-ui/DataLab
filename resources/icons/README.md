# Analysis icon semantics

All analysis icons use a `24x24` viewBox, a dark blue outline, and restrained
light-theme accent fills. The silhouettes are intentionally different by
method family so a menu remains scannable without reading every label.

## Statistical inference

- `descriptive.svg` — summary bars, mean marker, and spread range.
- `normality_test.svg` — bell curve with a fitted center line.
- `outlier_test.svg` — one extreme observation marked against a sample cloud.
- `correlation.svg` — paired observations with a rising association axis.
- `one_sample_t.svg` — one sample distribution tested against a reference.
- `two_sample_t.svg` — two separated sample distributions and comparison bar.
- `one_way_anova.svg` — three group means with a shared comparison bracket.
- `paired_t.svg` — linked before/after points for matched observations.
- `two_proportions.svg` — two proportion columns with a difference marker.
- `chi_square.svg` — contingency-table cells and chi-square crosshair.
- `mann_whitney.svg` — ranked dots separated into two ordered groups.
- `wilcoxon_signed_rank.svg` — signed ranks mirrored around zero.
- `kruskal_wallis.svg` — three ranked groups with a common rank baseline.
- `variance_test.svg` — two spread bands compared around a center line.
- `box_cox.svg` — curved transformation arrow turning skew into a bell.
- `logistic_regression.svg` — S-curve probability with binary endpoints.

## Regression and multivariate

- `regression.svg` — fitted line, residuals, and observed points.
- `pca.svg` — rotated principal axes through an observation cloud.
- `capability.svg` — process bell curve inside specification limits.
- `capability_sixpack.svg` — six compact diagnostic panels.

## Control charts

- `imr.svg` — individual points followed by a moving-range strip.
- `xbar_r.svg` — subgroup mean chart paired with a range chart.
- `xbar_s.svg` — subgroup mean chart paired with a standard-deviation chart.
- `p_chart.svg` — fraction-defective points against control limits.
- `np_chart.svg` — defective counts with a fixed center line.
- `c_chart.svg` — defect counts with a stepped count profile.
- `u_chart.svg` — defects-per-unit chart with changing exposure.
- `laney_p_chart.svg` — P chart with a variability-adjusted band.
- `laney_u_chart.svg` — U chart with a variability-adjusted band.
- `ewma.svg` — exponentially weighted trail smoothing noisy points.
- `cusum.svg` — cumulative deviation staircase crossing a decision line.

## Time series

- `time_series_smoothing.svg` — raw series under a smoothed overlay.
- `arima.svg` — forecast horizon extending from an observed series.
- `time_series_decomposition.svg` — stacked level, seasonal, and residual strips.
- `seasonal_forecasting.svg` — repeating seasonal wave with forecast cone.

## DOE, MSA, and graphics

- `two_factor_anova.svg` — two-factor interaction lines crossing by level.
- `doe_factorial.svg` — factorial design cube with corner runs.
- `gage_rr.svg` — crossed operator-by-part measurement matrix.
- `nested_gage_rr.svg` — nested measurement branches under operators.
- `attribute_agreement.svg` — repeated attribute ratings converging on agreement.
- `histogram.svg` — binned frequency columns and a distribution curve.
- `boxplot.svg` — median, quartiles, whiskers, and outlier.
- `pareto.svg` — descending bars with cumulative percentage curve.

## Data flow

- `import-data.svg` — arrow entering a table, representing data ingestion.
- `export-report.svg` — report page with an outward export arrow.
